📜Code of Ethics Draft

A code of ethics answers what we will do, or refuse to do, to stay true to our core values when things get tough. In your code of ethics:

  • Emphasize “do” language (verbs like protect, avoid, ensure, provide, disclose).
  • Include at least one policy about customers, one about employees, and one about the community or environment.
  • Reference dilemmas from the Ethics Stress Test (“In our industry, the main temptation is…”).

✅Example Code of Ethics

Core ValueEthical Practice (Statement in Code of Ethics)
IntegrityWe will be truthful in all advertising and product claims.
RespectWe will treat employees, customers, and suppliers with dignity and fairness.
AccountabilityWe will admit mistakes quickly and take corrective action.
SustainabilityWe will minimize waste and choose environmentally responsible suppliers.
TransparencyWe will make our pricing, sourcing, and data policies public.
